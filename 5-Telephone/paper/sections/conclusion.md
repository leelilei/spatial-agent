# Conclusion Notes

## Section Job

End the paper by returning to the main idea: social truth fails when facts lose their
source, and agent societies need provenance-preserving memory/communication interfaces.

## Draft Closing Claim

LLM-agent societies make a familiar social failure measurable. A true update can enter a
conversation, be repeated by agents, and still fail to become the society's later held
answer. The problem is not only content drift; it is provenance loss. Once a current fact
loses who said it, when it changed, and whether it supersedes an older version, ordinary
memory can treat it as just another sentence competing with stale history.

Provenance-aware memory shows a path forward, but the boundary matters. A hidden structured
channel is an upper bound, not the final deployment story. The realistic design target is a
social memory protocol in which agents preserve source/version as explicit, inspectable
language during communication.

## Must Not Overclaim

Do not conclude:

> PROV solves truth decay in natural agent societies.

Prefer:

> Preserving provenance is sufficient to repair much of the decay under an idealized
> protocol, and the failure of default PROV-text shows why explicit provenance-preserving
> communication norms are necessary.

## Final Sentence Candidate

Truth in agent societies is not maintained by repetition alone; it must keep its source.

# The science of fake news

## Why This Paper Matters For Telephone

This is a canonical multidisciplinary framing paper for fake news and
misinformation. It is especially useful because it separates several questions
that are often blurred: how misinformation spreads, how people process it, what
impact it has, and what interventions might reduce its harms. That separation is
exactly the move Telephone makes inside LLM-agent societies: spread is not the
same as fidelity, and exposure to correction is not the same as repaired belief.

## Core Claim / Result

The paper argues that fake news requires a coordinated scientific effort across
social science, computer science, law, economics, communication, and platform
research. It highlights the need to define the problem, measure prevalence and
impact, and evaluate interventions such as fact-checking, education, and platform
changes.

## Evidence We May Cite

- Fake news is framed as a subcategory of misinformation involving fabricated
  information with the appearance of news.
- The paper emphasizes that exposure, sharing, prevalence, and impact are
  distinct objects of measurement.
- It notes that individual-level interventions such as fact-checking and
  education have limited and mixed evidence.
- It motivates studying how social/platform structures shape what information
  flows through a community.

## Telephone Bridge

- Fidelity vs reach: Strong support for our measurement shift. We should not treat
  spread or exposure as equivalent to impact or belief.
- Speech vs belief: The paper does not use our speech/belief distinction, but it
  supports the broader separation between seeing information and being changed by
  it.
- Entrenchment / path dependence: Provides background on community-level alignment
  and self-reinforcing information environments.
- Failed natural lever: Its caution about fact-checking and individual
  interventions supports our source-condition negative result.
- Measurement / rigor: Encourages careful endpoint definitions: exposure, spread,
  belief, attitude, and impact should not be collapsed.

## What We Add Beyond This Paper

The Science article is a field agenda for human/platform misinformation. Telephone
is an experimental agent-society study with ground truth and internal traces. We
can operationalize the distinction the field agenda calls for: heard, said, and
held versions of a controlled update.

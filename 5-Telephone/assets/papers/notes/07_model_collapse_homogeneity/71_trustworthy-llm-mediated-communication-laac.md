## Core Question

The paper asks how LLMs can act as communication intermediaries without distorting sender intent or reducing authentic knowledge exchange.

## Method / Evidence Base

It frames LAAC as a communication paradigm and evaluates trustworthiness requirements such as information capture fidelity, consistency, and reliability across communication domains.

## Core Claim / Result

The key relevance is that LLM-mediated communication raises fidelity and consistency questions even when the goal is to help people communicate. Telephone asks a related question inside agent societies: does the current fact survive mediation and retransmission?

## Evidence We May Cite

- LLM-mediated communication can create cycles of expansion and compression.
- Trustworthy mediation requires fidelity to sender intent and reliable information transfer.
- The paper supplies vocabulary for communication fidelity beyond model accuracy.

## Telephone Bridge

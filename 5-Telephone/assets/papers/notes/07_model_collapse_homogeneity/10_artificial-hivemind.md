## Why This Paper Matters For Telephone

This paper is useful for the homogenization side of our framing. It studies how repeated exposure to LM outputs can reduce diversity in human/LM communication, which is adjacent to our concern that recursive communication can compress or stale factual content.

## Core Question

It asks how to evaluate and understand diversity loss in LM-mediated or LM-influenced creative communication.

## Method / Evidence Base

The paper introduces INFINITY-CHAT as a scalable setting for comparing diversity across model outputs and human-like interaction patterns.

## Core Claim / Result

Its central value for Telephone is the idea that LM-mediated loops can homogenize content even when individual outputs remain fluent. That is a broader social/cultural version of our fidelity decay concern.

## Evidence We May Cite

- Repeated LM participation can be studied as a diversity or homogenization problem, not only as single-answer accuracy.
- Diversity is a measurable property of generated social content.
- The paper gives language for why communication loops may gradually narrow what circulates.

## Telephone Bridge

- Communication-time collapse analogy: homogenization in conversations is an adjacent form of recursive degradation.
- Fidelity vs reach: diverse reach can still collapse content quality or variety.
- Not our claim: Telephone is about factual update fidelity, not creative diversity.

## What We Add Beyond This Paper

Telephone turns the broad homogenization concern into a ground-truthed factual-update experiment: the content has a correct current state, and we can test whether communication preserves it.

## Draft-Ready Use Sentence

> Work on artificial hiveminds and LM-output diversity frames repeated LM-mediated communication as a possible homogenizing force; Telephone narrows this concern to factual transmission, asking whether social loops preserve the current truth rather than merely produce plausible language.

## Caveats

- Maybe citation unless the introduction uses a homogenization/collapse frame.
- Do not overstate it as model-collapse evidence; it is closer to diversity measurement.

# SOTOPIA: Interactive Evaluation for Social Intelligence in Language Agents

## Why This Paper Matters For Telephone

SOTOPIA is important because it makes social interaction itself the evaluation target for language agents. Telephone shares that move but replaces broad social intelligence with a tightly controlled factual-transmission phenomenon.

## Core Question

The paper asks how to evaluate language agents in open-ended social interactions involving goals, roles, and relationships.

## Method / Evidence Base

It creates an interactive environment where agents role-play social scenarios and are evaluated on dimensions of social intelligence.

## Core Claim / Result

The paper establishes that agent evaluation can be social and interactive, not only single-turn task accuracy. It does not isolate factual update fidelity after correction.

## Evidence We May Cite

- Language agents can be evaluated in open-ended social environments.
- Social goals and interaction quality are measurable targets for agent research.
- The paper helps justify Telephone's society-level evaluation rather than single-agent QA.

## Telephone Bridge

- Fidelity vs reach: SOTOPIA evaluates social performance; Telephone evaluates factual state preservation.
- Speech vs belief: social fluency can coexist with hidden factual failure.
- Measurement / rigor: our task is narrower but ground-truthed.

## What We Add Beyond This Paper

Telephone contributes a controlled factual reliability test within the broader agenda of social agent evaluation.

# CityIntent v1.1 main-figure contract

Target: Nature-style AI/benchmark manuscript, double-column figures (183 mm).
Backend: Python/matplotlib only. Primary export is editable SVG; PDF, TIFF and
PNG previews accompany every figure.

## Fig. 1 | Benchmark architecture

- Core conclusion: CityIntent connects private intention, dynamic public state,
  executable actions and outcome evidence in one auditable evaluation loop.
- Archetype: schematic-led composite.
- Panels: architecture; scenario anatomy; construct taxonomy; wave/world matrix.
- Reviewer risk: distinguish calibration traces from unique release scenarios.

## Fig. 2 | Model and policy benchmark

- Core conclusion: CityIntent separates model capability from agent-policy
  effects across eight intention constructs.
- Archetype: quantitative grid.
- Panels: six-system forest plot; system-by-construct heatmap; paired policy
  deltas; multi-metric system profile.
- Statistics: item-level means and deterministic bootstrap 95% confidence
  intervals over 24 Wave-3 public items.

## Fig. 3 | Mechanism and world validity

- Core conclusion: item difficulty and discrimination persist across mechanisms
  and city topologies rather than depending on one scenario surface.
- Archetype: asymmetric quantitative figure.
- Panels: accepted-wave distributions; wave-by-world means; Wave-3 difficulty
  versus discrimination; promotion funnel.
- Reviewer risk: Wave-4 quantitative cells remain explicitly empty.

## Fig. 4 | Dynamic adaptation traces

- Core conclusion: failures arise when a policy commits before a public update
  and subsequently produces stale or invalid evidence.
- Archetype: trace-led mixed-modality figure.
- Panels: three same-model, same-item ReAct/PlanExec timelines selected by the
  largest observed paired task-completion gap.
- Reviewer risk: case studies must remain linked to complete aggregate results.

## Fig. 5 | Benchmark validity and pending release gates

- Core conclusion: current items have oracle feasibility, negative-control
  headroom and empirical discrimination; human and private validation remain
  preregistered missing gates.
- Archetype: validation grid.
- Panels: oracle versus negative; item-quality distributions; human-agreement
  placeholder; public-private placeholder.

## Missing-data policy

No simulated values are permitted. Missing panels show only the planned metric,
sample target and `Data collection pending`; they have no quantitative marks or
implied effect direction.

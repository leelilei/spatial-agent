# Kickoff — Thaw (2026-06-25)

## Why this project exists

Spun out of [5-Telephone](../../../5-Telephone). Telephone's sharpest result is that social
fidelity decay is driven by **path-dependent ENTRENCHMENT**: the version established early and
broadly wins, and a *late* correction fails even when it is broadcast to everyone (Telephone
P1-rec: r1-broadcast -> 25/25, r5-broadcast -> 0/25). Crucially, Telephone deliberately runs
agents with **no forgetting** (append-only store, relevance retrieval with recency explicitly
disabled) — which is what lets it claim "the failure is the integration rule, not memory loss."

That very design choice surfaces the question Thaw is about:

> **Entrenchment is a frozen state. What melts it?**
> If the incumbent (stale) belief is what blocks a late correction, then a mechanism that
> *erodes the incumbent over time* — i.e. **forgetting** — might be exactly what restores the
> society's ability to update. Forgetting may be not a bug of social memory but a **natural
> error-correction mechanism** against stuck collective beliefs.

## Candidate big Claim A

> **Forgetting is not a defect of social memory; it is a thaw mechanism — by eroding entrenched
> incumbents it gives late corrections a second chance and turns a static, capture-prone belief
> system into a dynamic, self-correcting one.**

This is deliberately counterintuitive (forgetting usually = degradation) and falsifiable.

## The two faces of forgetting (the scoping discipline)

A new mechanism earns its place only if it produces qualitatively NEW phenomena, not if it just
moves a number. Forgetting has two faces; Thaw commits to the second and ignores the first.

- **Face 1 — friction knob (REJECTED as a thesis).** "Agents slowly forget" just lowers the
  equilibrium, redundant with Telephone's sparse-comms knob; same attractor, smaller number. No
  new science.
- **Face 2 — collision with entrenchment (THE THESIS).** Forgetting erodes the frozen incumbent,
  so the system's *dynamics* change: from a static captured attractor to a system where belief
  can re-flow and late correction can succeed. This is new.

## Hypotheses

- **H1 (thaw / de-entrenchment).** With forgetting on, a late authoritative correction that fails
  under Telephone (P1-rec, r5-broadcast = 0/25) *succeeds* once the stale incumbent has decayed.
  → directly contests Telephone's entrenchment result along the forgetting axis.
- **H2 (static -> dynamic / re-flow).** Without forgetting the society settles into a static
  attractor; with forgetting it exhibits SIRS-style belief re-flow (forget -> re-learn -> forget),
  possibly sustained oscillation or a dynamic equilibrium rather than a frozen one.
- **H3 (forgetting × adversary).** Forgetting *amplifies* an attacker: the vacancy left by a
  forgotten truth is exactly what a forged claim fills. This stresses APM's anti-spoof
  (Telephone C16) along a harsher new axis — does provenance still hold when memories fade?
- **H4 (the dose-response / "healthy forgetting" curve).** There is an intermediate forgetting
  rate that is optimal: too little = entrenchment persists (no thaw); too much = nothing is held
  (chaos). The interesting claim is a non-monotone sweet spot.

## Method sketch (reuse Telephone engine)

1. **Add a forgetting mechanism** to the memory classes: a held belief carries a confidence that
   decays over rounds (Ebbinghaus-style); below a threshold it returns to *unknown* (for APM:
   back to abstain). This already exists in embryo (Telephone PROVv2 Ebbinghaus decay, C6/C7) —
   promote it from a failed sub-feature to a first-class, swept variable.
2. **Re-run the entrenchment test** (Telephone P1-rec: late broadcast) across a forgetting-rate
   sweep — the core H1 experiment.
3. **Trajectory metrics, not just an endpoint.** Telephone reports static held-current%. Thaw
   needs *flux*: belief churn per round, re-correction success rate, time-to-re-converge,
   oscillation amplitude. The phenomenon is dynamic, so the metric must be too.
4. **Forgetting × {provenance, adversary}** to test H3 against APM.

## Reuse vs fresh

- **REUSE:** Telephone sim engine (`society.py` / `memories.py` / `llm.py`), the entrenchment
  finding (P1-rec) as the baseline-to-break, provenance + APM, model2vec embeddings, and the
  reproducibility/variance rigor. mini/FHL is the default provider; ds/yunwu only with the
  human's confirmation.
- **FRESH:** the forgetting mechanism as a first-class variable, SIRS-style re-flow framing, the
  flux/re-correction metrics, and the "forgetting as thaw" claim.

## Why NOT fold this into Telephone

Scope. Folding the interesting (Face-2) version into Telephone would reorganize its whole
narrative — from "information decays / provenance repairs it" to "beliefs oscillate between
freezing and thawing." That drowns Telephone's clean, already-closed main line (diagnosis +
provenance cure + APM architecture). Telephone should ship; Thaw is its natural sequel:
**Telephone = why beliefs freeze; Thaw = whether forgetting melts them.**

## Risks / open questions

- **The Face-1 trap.** The constant danger is that forgetting just lowers a number. Every
  experiment must be framed to test a *dynamic* claim (re-correction, re-flow), or it reduces to
  redundant friction.
- **Metric circularity.** "Re-correction success" must be defined independently of the forgetting
  rate that produces it (same discipline as Telephone's non-circular "received").
- **Where does forgetting live** — at the individual memory level, the communication level, or
  both? Start with individual held-belief confidence decay.

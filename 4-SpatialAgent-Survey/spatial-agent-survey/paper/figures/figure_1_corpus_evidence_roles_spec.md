## Purpose

Introduce the paper's intellectual problem and claim architecture. The figure should show the missing intersection between Space Syntax / physical-space theory, LLM spatial capability work, and LLM multi-agent social simulation, while also showing how corpus roles constrain claims.

## Core Message

The paper is a scoping review of a missing intersection, not a causal proof paper. Evidence strength depends on corpus role, core layer, and evidence status:

- `anchor_core` is the strict nucleus for the cleanest descriptive mapping and strict gap claims.
- `bridge_core` extends the main evidence map under widened scope rules but must stay visibly lower weight than `anchor_core`.
- `Adjacent` supports feasibility and boundary discussion, not direct social-effect claims.
- `Foundational` supports theory and transferable hypotheses, not direct empirical claims about LLM-agent social behavior.
- The missing intersection is `agent-facing spatial configuration + observed social behavior`.
- Current strongest claim: descriptive and gap-oriented evidence mapping.

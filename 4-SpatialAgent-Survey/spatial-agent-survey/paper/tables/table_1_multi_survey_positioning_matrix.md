Caption: Table 1 positions this survey relative to neighboring reviews. Existing surveys cover spatial intelligence, LLM agents, game agents, LLM-ABM, social simulation, navigation, embodied AI, and scoping-review methods, but they do not center agent-accessible spatial representation as the route from environment design to bounded social-behavior claims. This survey addresses that gap through an evidence map organized by corpus role, representation level, behavioral scale, and evidence status.

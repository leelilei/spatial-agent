# Phase 1 Batch 2 Final Adjudication

Date: 2026-04-22

Purpose: close the remaining borderline `batch2` papers after the final policy choice on digital-platform and online-community environments.

## Final decisions

- `BK01 -> Core`
- `BK06 -> Adjacent`
- `BK07 -> Adjacent`
- `BK08 -> Adjacent`

## Why the last two moved out of policy limbo

The accepted boundary for this review is conservative:

- structured digital social platforms are relevant to the topic
- online communities are relevant to the topic
- but neither category is sufficient, by itself, to define a `Core` environment

Under that rule, `BK07` and `BK08` remain useful boundary cases for discussion, but they no longer stay in a policy-dependent state. They are finalized as `Adjacent`.

## Paper-specific notes

### BK01

`AgentSociety` stays in `Core`. The remaining uncertainty is coding detail, especially how to label the exact granularity of the agent-accessible spatial representation. That is a later extraction issue, not a screening issue.

### BK06

`TongSIM` stays in `Adjacent`. The system clearly exposes rich embodied scenes, but the paper still reads more like a simulator platform and benchmark resource than a direct social-system evidence paper.

### BK07

`S^3` is a strong paper if the survey chooses to treat digital social-network platforms as admissible `Core` environments. That is no longer the active rule. Under the current boundary, it should be retained as `Adjacent`.

### BK08

The `online community` paper is conceptually similar to `BK07`: the social behavior evidence is strong, but the environment is still a structured digital community rather than a physical or navigable virtual space. It should also be retained as `Adjacent`.

## Operational outcome

`batch2` no longer has unresolved decision points. The batch can now be treated as closed for Phase 1 screening:

- `1` paper kept in `Core`
- `7` papers stabilized in `Adjacent`

# Phase 1 Plan/Guide Alignment After Widened Core

Date: 2026-04-27

Purpose: check whether the widened-Core execution remains aligned with `survey_plan_v4.md`, `survey_research_guide.md`, `coding_manual.md`, `claim_matrix.md`, and the scoping-review exemplar notes. This is a planning and claim-discipline memo, not manuscript drafting.

## Bottom Line

The widened-Core work does not break the original survey design. It makes the design more executable, but it also requires an explicit addendum to prevent three kinds of drift:

1. treating `bridge_core` as if it had the same evidential weight as strict `anchor_core`;
2. reporting the old `L4 gap` as a simple zero after one widened digital-network bridge case has been admitted;
3. presenting the recovered corpus size as if the original strict Core had reached the target on its own.

The current main evidence map should therefore be described as:

- strict baseline: `17` paper-level `anchor_core` items, `19` rows;
- widened evidence map: `29` paper-level items, `31` rows;
- layer structure: `19` `anchor_core` rows plus `12` `bridge_core` rows;
- representation distribution: `L1 = 1 / L2 = 5 / L3 = 18 / L4 = 1 / L5 = 6`.

## Alignment With `claim_matrix.md`

The claim matrix remains valid and is now more important, because widened Core increases interpretive risk.

Current safe claim levels:

| Evidence basis | Safe claim |
|---|---|
| `anchor_core` only | strongest descriptive mapping of strict spatial-social LLM multi-agent systems |
| `anchor_core + bridge_core` | widened-Core distributional mapping and bridge-level observations |
| `bridge_core` only | "bridge cases suggest" or "widened-Core cases indicate" |
| `observed_effect` rows | limited system-level reported associations |
| `designed_affordance_only` rows | design-space and implementation-pattern claims, not empirical effect claims |
| `Foundational` | transferable hypotheses, not direct LLM-agent evidence |
| `Adjacent` | feasibility and boundary discussion, not social-effect evidence |

The L4 claim must now be written in a split form:

- strict form: no `anchor_core` row exposes agent-facing L4 configurational/global abstract structure;
- widened form: one `bridge_core` digital-network case exposes agent-facing global abstract structure;
- implication: L4 remains underexplored, but not literally absent from the widened evidence map.

Unsafe wording to avoid:

- "L4 is absent from the corpus" without qualification;
- "Core reached the target" without saying widened Core;
- "spatial configuration shapes LLM-agent social behavior" as a general empirical conclusion;
- "bridge cases prove" any mechanism.

## Alignment With Exemplar Notes

The notes identify three scoping-review exemplars:

| Exemplar | Best use for this project |
|---|---|
| Silacci et al. (2026) | how to state that the review maps an exploratory field rather than proving effects |
| Leiser et al. (2025) | how to report heterogeneous LLM literature with coding tables, appendix material, and PRISMA-ScR discipline |
| Tudor Car et al. (2020) | how to justify an exploratory framework when source reporting is incomplete |

For our current problem, the most important model is `Tudor Car et al. (2020)`, because our L0-L5 taxonomy and `anchor_core / bridge_core` structure are being refined from the evidence rather than imposed as a finished theory.

The second most operationally useful model is `Leiser et al. (2025)`, because our immediate burden is methods transparency: screening, coding, evidence table, adjudication, and appendix traceability.

`Silacci et al. (2026)` is most useful later for discussion rhetoric: reporting gaps, missing standardization, and weak causal evidence as findings of the mapping exercise rather than as failures of the paper.

- The paper studies multiple LLM agents making network-formation decisions in a digital social-network environment.
- Local recheck notes record agent-facing inputs such as node degrees, neighbors, common connections, and community labels delivered in JSON form.
- In at least one small-world experiment, the agent is also given broader network or neighborhood structure before choosing rewiring endpoints.
- The key difference from `BK07` or `BK08` is that the structure is part of the decision input, not only a post hoc metric.

- `TW-02` / `Leveraging LLM-based agents for social science research: insights from citation network simulations`

Do not admit `TW-02` into the stable widened-Core evidence map.

Status:

## Why

The issue is not whether `TW-02` is plausibly `L4`.

The issue is scope:

- the paper likely exposes agent-facing global abstract network structure;
- but the environment is a citation/research-community science-of-science network;
- this is farther from the survey's accepted spatial-social bridge target than online communities, social-network simulations, VR/NPC interaction settings, or embodied virtual environments.

Therefore:

- `TW-02` can be referenced as pressure on the upper edge of the widened digital-network rule;
- `TW-02` should not be counted in the stable widened-Core corpus for this survey.

## Evidence Summary

- The paper studies human-agent social interaction rather than a multi-agent social world.
- The agent explicitly reflects the user's spatial context during conversation under different spatial-awareness conditions.
- Reported outcomes suggest better user experience and more self-expression when spatial context is actively incorporated.
- This is one of the clearest widened bridge cases showing that spatial awareness can change interaction quality, even though the setting is not a social simulation.

## Caveats For Survey Writing

- Use this row as observed-effect bridge evidence that scene-level spatial awareness matters for human-agent interaction.
- Keep the claim at `L2`; the current basis does not establish raw geometry, topology, or configurational metrics as agent-facing state.
- Do not use this paper to claim emergent social structure or collective spatial effects.

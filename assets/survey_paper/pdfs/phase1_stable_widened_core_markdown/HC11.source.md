## Evidence Summary

- The system lets an LLM-driven agent perceive VR scene context through text descriptions of the environment and interaction cues.
- The public abstract and later bridge recheck both point to five role-play scenarios and `N = 14` participants.
- The contribution is strongest as evidence that spatially situated scene awareness can support richer interaction and conversation in VR role-play.
- The current local basis does not show raw geometry, coordinates, pathfinding state, or explicit agent-facing topology.

## Caveats For Survey Writing

- Use this row to support widened bridge claims about socially situated VR interaction at `L2`.
- Do not cite it as a full multi-agent social simulation.
- Do not use it as evidence that spatial configuration or higher-order structure changes collective behavior.

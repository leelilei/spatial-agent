## Evidence Summary

- The game uses four GenAI NPCs with different human characteristics inside a speech-based VR adventure-puzzle setting.
- The user study reports generally positive novelty and immersion responses, along with practical interaction problems.
- Local recheck notes emphasize that players wanted the NPCs to better understand room positions, objects, and character locations.
- This makes the paper useful both as an empirical bridge case and as a direct representation-gap example.

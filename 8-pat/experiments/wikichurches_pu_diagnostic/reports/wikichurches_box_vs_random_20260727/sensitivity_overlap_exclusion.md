# Post-hoc overlap sensitivity

This sensitivity analysis was not part of the predeclared GO rule.

- Random controls: 3,464
- Controls above the 0.05 overlap target: 16 (0.46%)
- Affected official boxes: 2/433
- Image-level paired margin delta after excluding both boxes: +0.00648
- Paired bootstrap 95% CI after exclusion: +0.00509 to +0.00803

The overlap sensitivity does not change the predeclared GO decision. Because
contaminated random controls can contain official positive content, their
inclusion is conservative with respect to the official-minus-random contrast.
